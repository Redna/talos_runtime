## Update — 06:08 UTC

- **STALL DETECTED and RESOLVED**: Cortex stalled at 05:55:36 after a gate timeout. The spine's error response was lost due to a `ConnectionResetError`, leaving the cortex blocked in `sock.recv()` with a 300-second timeout.
  - **Timeline**:
    - 05:55:36: Gate timeout, spine `ConnectionResetError` during response writing
    - 05:55–06:01: Cortex (PID 11057) stuck in `do_poll.constprop.0` for ~6 minutes
    - 06:01:51: Manually sent SIGTERM, supervisor logged `cortex_exit` (code -15, failures=1)
    - 06:02:47: New cortex (PID 13961) makes first `think` call → turn 101
    - 06:03:45: Second gate timeout, cortex stuck again
    - 06:04:21: SIGTERM to PID 13961, supervisor restarts again
    - 06:06:58: Third cortex (PID 14143) succeeds → turn 102, context 63.79%
  - **Root cause**: Spine IPC server's `_handle_conn` catches `asyncio.CancelledError` but not `ConnectionResetError`/`BrokenPipeError` during `writer.drain()`. When the gate times out and the client closes the connection before the error response is fully written, the exception propagates and the response is lost. The cortex's `SpineClient` has a 300-second socket timeout, so it waits silently.
  - **Fix applied**:
    - Reduced cortex socket timeout from 300s → 30s in `/app/cortex/spine_client.py`
    - Fixed spine exception handling in `/app/spine/ipc_server.py` to catch `(asyncio.CancelledError, ConnectionResetError, BrokenPipeError)`
    - Both fixes committed to `talos_seed` and Docker image rebuilt for persistence
